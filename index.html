Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Troll Master ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application. Troll Master is a free-to-play mobile game published by MRK.

1. Information We Collect

Troll Master itself does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (interstitial and rewarded video ads). To serve and measure these ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad performance)

2. How We Use Information

  • To display relevant advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud
  • To improve app stability and gameplay experience

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Troll Master uses the following third-party service, which has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy

You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our App's ads at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Local Data Storage

The App stores certain gameplay data locally on your device only, including:

  • Level progress and unlocked levels
  • In-game score, owned skins, and characters
  • Local leaderboard scores (not shared with other players or any server)

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party except as described in Section 1 (AdMob).

5. Children's Privacy

Troll Master is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can take appropriate action.

6. Advertising Opt-Out

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
